### 01. Time Series Forecasting of Brazilian Academic Production

**Scientific Data Analysis | Deep Learning | Forecasting**

A scientometric research project investigating the evolution of Brazilian scientific production through temporal modeling and predictive methods.

- Analysis of academic production across eight research areas.
- Construction and analysis of historical time series (1990–2024).
- Application of Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) architectures.
- Nonlinear regression and forecasting methodologies.
- Analysis of scientific publications, research projects, and academic supervision.
- Data integration from Lattes and OpenAlex.

**Recognition:** Honorable Mention for Best Research Project in the Communication and Information category — XV UFABC/USCS Undergraduate Research Meeting (2025).

### 02. Symbolic Regression Benchmark

**Automated Mathematical Discovery | Evolutionary Computation | Optimization**

A systematic experimental benchmark evaluating symbolic regression algorithms for mathematical expression discovery.

- Comparative evaluation of multiple symbolic regression algorithms.
- Hyperparameter optimization using Optuna.
- Five-fold cross-validation and multiple random seeds.
- Evaluation under different mathematical operator configurations.
- Reproducible experimental pipelines.
- Comparison of predictive accuracy, complexity, and model interpretability.

Algorithms under investigation include Operon, PySR, GOMEA, GSGP, GPZDG, QLattice, uDSR, TPSR, LLM-SR, DySymNet, and RAG-SR.

### 03. Academic Collaboration Network Analysis

**Network Science | Graph Theory | Data Analysis**

Computational investigation of scientific collaboration networks within an academic research environment.

- Construction of collaboration networks.
- Graph representation and network analysis using NetworkX.
- Community detection through Louvain clustering.
- Degree and weighted-degree centrality.
- Maximal clique analysis.
